# FUTURE.md

Ideas deliberately out of scope for the MVP. Not commitments.

- **Browser extension** — detect the tab the user drifts to and pre-fill the drift reason.
- **Website blocking** — optional blocklist enforced during an active session.
- **Accountability partner** — share a daily summary with one other person.
- **Cloud sync** — carry tasks/sessions across devices.
- **Analytics history** — week/month trends, best time of day, drift patterns over time.
- **AI coaching** — surface patterns ("you drift ~18 min in") without lecturing.
- **Calendar integration** — pull the day's blocks in as candidate tasks.
- **Mobile app** — native timer with lock-screen controls.
- **Desktop app** — always-on-top mini timer.
- **Website usage detection** — passive drift capture instead of a manual button.
